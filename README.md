#  Global Data Science Salaries & Employment Intelligence Dashboard

An interactive and comprehensive data visualization project built using **Tableau**, analyzing global data science compensation drivers, employment patterns, experience tiers, and geographic hiring distribution.

---

##  Project Overview
The data science market is expanding rapidly. This project dives deep into global salary data to uncover what truly drives compensation and hiring trends worldwide, transforming raw workforce data into clear, actionable visual insights.

---

##  Key Insights & Findings

- **Compensation Drivers:** Salaries scale significantly from Intermediate to Senior and Expert levels. Notably, expert-level contract roles command some of the highest average rates across the dataset.
- **Employment Structure:** Full-time roles overwhelmingly dominate the market at **96.87%**, while freelance and part-time opportunities account for less than 3% combined.
- **Hiring by Company Size:** Medium-sized enterprises lead hiring volume at **53.71%**, followed by large organizations at **32.62%** and small businesses at **13.67%**.
- **Experience Breakdown:** Senior-level professionals make up the largest share at **46.13%**, followed by Intermediate talent at **35.09%**, highlighting strong enterprise demand for established technical expertise.
- **Geographic Distribution:** The United States represents the largest share of employee residence, followed closely by Great Britain (GB), India (IN), Canada (CA), and Germany (DE).

---

##  Tech Stack & Implementation

### 1. Data Preparation & Transformation
- Cleaned and structured multi-attribute workforce datasets to ensure accurate salary scaling and categorization.

### 2. Tableau Visualization Features
- **Dynamic Filtering & Segmentation:** Filtering by experience levels, employment types, company sizes, and geographic regions.
- **Comparative Salary Analysis:** Visualizing compensation gaps across seniority tiers and contracting structures.
- **Geographic & Structural Breakdowns:** Highlighting global employee distribution and company hiring preferences.

### 3. UI/UX Design
- Designed with a clean, modern, and executive-ready visual layout optimized for professional data storytelling and intuitive exploration.
